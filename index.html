About Modunex

Modunex is a Western Australian leader in structural, mechanical and piping modelling and detailing, delivering innovative, project-driven solutions to the region’s most complex industrial challenges.

Based in Perth, Modunex is built on a foundation of integrity, customer focus, and long-term collaborative partnerships. Our expertise spans Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 projects, with adaptable solutions at any scale.
