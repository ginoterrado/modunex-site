Civil Drafting

Our drafting division runs on the major engineering platforms to produce accurate 3D models, fabrication drawings and project documentation for structural, mechanical and piping work.

How we work

Tekla and Bentley ProStructures carry the complex structural and steel detailing, with coordination checks that find clashes before anything reaches the workshop.

Inventor and SolidWorks cover mechanical systems and components. Revit supports BIM workflows so architectural and engineering models stay aligned.

AutoCAD underpins 2D drafting and documentation, and Navisworks handles clash detection and model coordination on multi-trade projects.

Across the project lifecycle

  • Feasibility studies and concept design at the earliest planning stages.
  • Coordinated 3D models developed as the design firms up.
  • Shop drawings issued for fabrication and installation.
