About Modunex

A Western Australian leader in structural, mechanical and piping modelling and detailing.

Who we are

Modunex is a Western Australian leader in structural, mechanical and piping modelling and detailing, delivering innovative, project-driven solutions to the region’s most complex industrial challenges. Based in Perth, we are built on a foundation of integrity, customer focus, and long-term collaborative partnerships.

Our team’s expertise extends across Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 projects, which means adaptable solutions at any scale or complexity.

With extensive experience in 3D laser scanning, point cloud processing and advanced modelling using Bentley ProStructures, Tekla, Revit and AutoCAD, we capture as-built conditions and produce accurate, coordinated models for remediation, upgrades and new construction.

Our portfolio includes projects in oil and gas, mining, and infrastructure, where we deliver general arrangement and fabrication drawings, isometric layouts and comprehensive bills of materials to support construction and procurement. From concept design through to final engineering, our commitment to regulatory compliance, safety and operational excellence holds throughout.
