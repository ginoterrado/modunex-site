Point cloud capture and processing

Scan data is cleaned, aligned and brought directly into our design models, giving an accurate picture of existing site conditions before design work begins.

Working from measured reality rather than assumed dimensions is what removes risk from a brownfield job. It is the difference between a part that fits and a part that comes back.

Where it applies

  • As-built capture ahead of remediation or upgrade design.
  • Verification of existing conditions before fabrication commits.
  • Integration of client-supplied scan data into a working model.
