6MW Fast Charger Loadbank Switchroom

Fortescue Zero

As-built 3D modelling for a 6MW fast charger loadbank switchroom, part of Fortescue Zero’s heavy electric vehicle charging programme. The switchroom supports factory acceptance testing and validation of a world-first 6MW charger designed to charge 240-tonne battery electric mining trucks in under 30 minutes. Our structural and mechanical modelling captured the constructed condition accurately, in line with Fortescue’s zero-emission mining objectives.

Yara Drop Down Stair

A complete detailing service for custom hydraulic drop-down access stairs, covering 3D modelling, general arrangement drawings and shop detailing to fabrication standard.

  • 3D modelling of the hydraulic drop-down stair, including cylinder arrangements, hinges, locking mechanisms, safety rails and structural interfaces for coordination and clash detection.
  • General arrangement drawings showing plans, elevations, sections, clearances, key dimensions, levels and connection points for review and approval.
  • Shop detailing with coordinated stair assemblies, mounting brackets, pins, plates, bolt lists, hydraulic component mounting details and material schedules.

EII Causeway Watermain

Karratha, Western Australia

  • Processed client-supplied 3D scan data to establish a precise as-built basis for design.
  • Developed a coordinated 3D model of the causeway watermain, including all critical fittings and interfaces.
  • Prepared general arrangement and isometric drawings for fabrication and installation.
  • Generated a detailed bill of materials for procurement and cost control.

Safe Access Upgrades — Loading and Unloading Ramp

Goldfields, Western Australia

  • Developed concept design for handrails, swing gates and a concrete stairway for MMO review.
  • Engineered final designs to comply with AS 1657 requirements for industrial access systems.
  • Produced hybrid steelwork and concrete drawings for combined structural and shop fabrication use.

Feedwell Roof Design and Feeder Cone Remediation

Gold Fields, Western Australia

  • Processed and back-modelled 3D scan data for the existing feedwell structure.
  • Reinforced the feedwell and added openings to meet operational requirements.
  • Developed a concept design for the feeder cone, addressing structural and maintenance needs.
  • Produced hybrid general arrangement drawings for combined structural and shop documentation.

KBT Sulphur Shed Remediation

Kwinana, Western Australia

  • Conducted comprehensive on-site 3D laser scanning to capture as-built conditions of the sulphur shed.
  • Processed point cloud data and developed an accurate 3D model in Bentley ProStructures.
  • Produced high-quality general arrangement drawings to support remediation planning and construction.
